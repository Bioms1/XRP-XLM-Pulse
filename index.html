Prices, 24h volume, market cap, 24h and 7-day change and the hourly 7-day chart for XRP, XLM, HBAR, QNT, ADA, SOL, ETH and BTC come from the free CoinGecko coins/markets endpoint (no key needed), polled every 30 seconds. CoinGecko itself refreshes roughly every 45 to 60 seconds, so some polls return unchanged numbers. Volume is aggregated across exchanges and cannot be split into buys and sells, so “traded” means total turnover.

Whole market figures (total market cap and its 24h change) come from CoinGecko’s free /global endpoint, checked every 2 minutes. The Fear & Greed score comes from the alternative.me Fear & Greed Index, a daily index checked every 30 minutes. If a source does not answer, that item shows Unknown.

Each coin’s activity ring compares its 24h volume (as a share of market cap) with a rough usual level for that coin, so 50 means a normal day. Those usual levels are estimates, not measured averages. Velocity is a heuristic: 60% average coin activity versus usual and 40% average absolute 24h price move. Zones: Quiet below 35, Normal 35 to 65, Busy above 65.
