Prices, 24h volume, market cap and 24h change come from the free CoinGecko simple/price endpoint (no key needed), refreshed every 30 seconds. Volume is aggregated across exchanges and cannot be split into buys and sells, so “traded” means total turnover.

The radar is an estimate from time of day and typical liquidity windows for Asia-Pacific, Europe and the Americas. It is not derived from on-chain data, which is pseudonymous.

Velocity is a heuristic: 60% volume-to-market-cap turnover and 40% average absolute 24h price move. Zones: Sluggish below 35, Steady 35 to 65, Explosive above 65.
